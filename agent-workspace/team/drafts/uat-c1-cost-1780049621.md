## Cost Differential

## Recommendation
Use Haiku for high-volume Director routing tasks where reasoning depth is secondary. Reserve Sonnet for complex multi-step decisions. At moderate volume (50M tokens/month), switching to Haiku saves ~$110/month on input alone.
