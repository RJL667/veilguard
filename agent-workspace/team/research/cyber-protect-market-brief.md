# Phishield Cyber Protect: Business vs Corporate — SA Market Brief

**Prepared by:** Phishield Research Team  
**Date:** 2025  
**Classification:** Internal — Team Knowledge

---

## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Phishield UMA (Pty) Ltd is South Africa's specialist digital risk underwriting manager, underwritten by Bryte Insurance Company Limited (FSP 17703). Its Cyber Protect suite addresses a market where the average cost of a data breach reached approximately R44.1 million in 2025, yet standalone cyber insurance uptake among SA businesses remains low — a structural protection gap confirmed by multiple industry sources ([Moonstone, 2025](https://www.moonstone.co.za/the-cost-of-standing-still-why-cyber-insurance-is-south-africas-missing-shield/)).

The suite splits cleanly across two tiers: **Cyber Protect Business** targets SMEs with standardised, entry-level cover from R686/month; **Cyber Protect Corporate** targets organisations with annual turnover above R200 million, offering bespoke, fully negotiated policy structures. Both products are broker-distributed and include 24/7 Cyber Emergency Response Team (CERT) access as a core service differentiator.

The SA competitive landscape is dominated by Santam and Hollard/iTOO, with OLEA Cyber SME emerging as a direct challenger in the SME segment. Global market conditions in 2025 favour buyers: premiums declined approximately 11% due to increased capacity ([Lockton, 2025](https://www.lockton.com/en/insights/cyber-insurance-market-update-rates-decline-despite-rising-claims)), though underwriting standards are tightening around security hygiene baselines. Phishield's strongest differentiator is its SA-native, cyber-specialist positioning and the bundled Funds Protect product — a coverage category most generalist competitors do not replicate.

## PHISHIELD CYBER PROTECT BUSINESS

### Scope of Cover

Cyber Protect Business is a standardised cyber liability policy designed for SMEs. Cover is structured across nine benefit categories with fixed sub-limits per tier:

| Benefit | R1m Tier | R2.5m Tier | R5m Tier |
|---|---|---|---|
| Business Interruption Loss | R1,000,000 | R2,500,000 | R5,000,000 |
| Multimedia Liability Claims | R1,000,000 | R2,500,000 | R5,000,000 |
| Regulatory Expenses & Penalties | R1,000,000 | R2,500,000 | R5,000,000 |
| Third Party Claims | R1,000,000 | R2,500,000 | R5,000,000 |
| Emergency Response Costs | R1,000,000 | R2,500,000 | R5,000,000 |
| Data Restoration Costs | R500,000 | R1,250,000 | R2,500,000 |
| Cyber Extortion Costs | R150,000 | R250,000 | R500,000 |
| PCI Fines & Penalties | R150,000 | R250,000 | R500,000 |
| Funds Protect (bundled) | R150,000 | R200,000 | R250,000 |

Premiums start from R686/month (R1m tier), R747/month (R2.5m tier), and R921/month (R5m tier). Cover limits of R10m, R25m, and R50m are available on application to quotes@phishield.com. Premiums and limits are subject to industry and annual turnover underwriting. A 6% monthly premium fee is collected by Bryte on behalf of Phishield UMA for CERT access, external cyber risk assessment scanning, 10 monthly bank account verifications, and specialised legal counsel during a cyber emergency.

Sources: [https://www.phishield.com](https://www.phishield.com); internal Cyber Protect Business Brochure 2025.

### Who It Is For

Any SME that uses computer systems to conduct business, holds client or sensitive data, transacts online, or holds a business bank account. The product is explicitly positioned as suitable for all SMEs regardless of sector, with the legal sector (law firms) specifically cited as a target vertical ([De Rebus](https://www.derebus.org.za)).

### Key Exclusions and Conditions

- Cover limits, premiums, and sub-limits vary by industry and annual turnover; acceptance is subject to underwriting.
- Funds Protect cover above R250,000 requires documented account verification procedures (vetting of new vendors/payees, beneficiary loading controls, amendment verification controls).
- Policy lapses on the last day of the month for which premium was received if a debit order remains unpaid; claims during unpaid periods are considered at Phishield's discretion only after outstanding premium is received.
- Standard cyber policy exclusions apply (war, intentional acts, pre-existing known incidents). Full terms in policy wording.

## PHISHIELD CYBER PROTECT CORPORATE

### Scope of Cover

Cyber Protect Corporate is a bespoke cyber liability solution for large organisations. There is no standardised schedule — policy structure, limits, sub-limits, and excesses are negotiated per client based on risk profile, group structure, and industry. Core coverage categories mirror the Business product but are expanded and customisable:

- **First-party operational losses:** Business interruption, system restoration, data recovery.
- **Incident response:** Dedicated CERT, forensic investigation, legal counsel, PR crisis management.
- **Regulatory response:** POPIA notification costs, regulatory fines and penalties (where insurable).
- **Third-party liability:** Network security liability, privacy liability, multimedia/media liability.
- **Cyber extortion:** Ransomware negotiation and payment costs.
- **Funds Protect integration:** Available as an add-on for payment fraud and BEC exposure.

Source: [https://www.phishield.com/cyber-protect-corporate](https://www.phishield.com/cyber-protect-corporate)

### Who It Is For

Organisations with annual turnover exceeding R200 million, including those with complex holding structures, cross-border operations, or significant sensitive data and digital infrastructure. The product is explicitly designed to accommodate group-level structures and industry-specific requirements. Healthcare entities and public administration bodies are noted exclusions from the standard Corporate product scope.

### Key Exclusions and Conditions

- No standard policy wording — each placement is bespoke; broker engagement is mandatory.
- Healthcare and public administration sectors are excluded from the standard Corporate product.
- Underwriting requires completion of Phishield's Cyber Risk Assessment Form (V1 2026), which evaluates data governance, backup practices, identity and access management, network and email security, MDR/SOC capability, asset inventory, and BCM/DR maturity.
- All Phishield products are underwritten by Bryte Insurance Company Limited (FSP 17703).

## COMPETITIVE LANDSCAPE

### 1. Santam Cyber Insurance

Santam is South Africa's largest short-term insurer and distributes cyber cover exclusively through licensed brokers. Its product covers first-party expenses (forensics, legal, data restoration), business interruption, cybercrime and extortion, confidentiality/privacy liability, media liability, and regulatory fines.

**Cover limits:** Up to R100 million or USD 5 million for SA/Africa operations; up to USD 10 million for businesses with international operations.  
**Pricing:** No public schedule; fully broker-quoted based on risk profile.  
**Gap vs Phishield:** Santam does not offer a bundled Funds Protect equivalent for payment fraud/BEC. Its SME entry point lacks the transparent published pricing that Phishield Business provides. Santam's brand and distribution network are significantly larger, giving it reach advantages.

Source: [https://www.santam.co.za/products/business/cyber-insurance/](https://www.santam.co.za/products/business/cyber-insurance/)

### 2. Hollard / iTOO Special Risks

Hollard distributes cyber insurance through its specialist underwriting arm, iTOO Special Risks, via a network of over 900 brokers. The iTOO Cyber product covers privacy and network security breaches, business interruption, data restoration, cyber extortion, and third-party liabilities. The **OLEA Cyber SME** product (underwritten by Hollard, managed by iTOO) is a direct SME-segment competitor to Phishield Business.

**Cover limits:** Not publicly disclosed; tailored per placement.  
**Pricing:** Not publicly disclosed.  
**Gap vs Phishield:** iTOO/OLEA does not publish entry-level pricing, making Phishield Business's transparent R686/month starting point a clear differentiator for cost-sensitive SMEs. Hollard's broader distribution and brand recognition are competitive advantages. No equivalent to Phishield's Funds Protect bundling is evident in public product descriptions.

Sources: [https://www.hollard.co.za/business-insurance/cyber-insurance](https://www.hollard.co.za/business-insurance/cyber-insurance); [https://itoo.co.za](https://itoo.co.za)

### 3. Bryte Insurance (Direct / Other UMAs)

Bryte Insurance Company Limited is Phishield's own underwriter and also distributes cyber cover through other channels and UMAs. This creates a structural dynamic where Phishield competes in the market using Bryte paper, while Bryte may also support competing UMA placements. Bryte's direct cyber offering targets data protection and cyber liability for SMEs.

**Gap vs Phishield:** Phishield's specialist cyber-only focus, CERT infrastructure, and Funds Protect bundling differentiate it from Bryte's broader, less specialised direct channel.

Source: [https://www.brytesa.com](https://www.brytesa.com)

### 4. Lockton / Global Capacity (Market Context)

International brokers such as Lockton place cyber risk for larger SA corporates with Lloyd's of London and global carrier capacity. In 2025, global cyber premium rates declined approximately 11% due to increased market capacity, even as claims frequency and severity rose ([Lockton, 2025](https://www.lockton.com/en/insights/cyber-insurance-market-update-rates-decline-despite-rising-claims)). This creates downward pricing pressure on Phishield Corporate placements competing against international capacity for large-turnover clients.

**Market context:** The average SA data breach cost reached R44.1 million in 2025. SA-specific risks — infrastructure instability, load-shedding, skills shortages — are forcing insurers to harden underwriting standards, particularly around MFA, incident response plans, and SOC/MDR capability ([Simah, 2025](https://simah.co.za/south-africas-unique-cybersecurity-and-ai-risk-landscape/)).

## BROKER POSITIONING

### Strengths

- **Transparent SME pricing.** Phishield Business publishes entry-level premiums (from R686/month), which no major competitor replicates publicly. This reduces friction in the SME sales cycle and enables brokers to quote indicatively without a full submission.
- **Funds Protect bundling.** The integration of payment fraud/BEC cover into the Cyber Protect suite is a genuine product differentiator. Competitors do not offer an equivalent bundled product, and BEC remains the highest-frequency cyber loss event for SA SMEs.
- **Cyber-specialist identity.** Phishield is the only SA UMA whose entire product portfolio is cyber-focused. This specialist positioning supports credibility with technically sophisticated buyers and enables deeper underwriting expertise than generalist insurers.
- **CERT as a service feature.** 24/7 emergency response team access is included in the premium (via the 6% service fee), not sold as an add-on. This is a tangible, marketable benefit.
- **Sophos partnership.** A preferential rate arrangement for businesses using Sophos security products ([ITWeb](https://www.itweb.co.za)) creates a channel into the managed security services market and rewards security-mature clients.

### Weaknesses

- **Distribution scale.** Phishield's broker network is smaller than Santam's or Hollard's. Market reach is constrained relative to generalist competitors with established national broker relationships.
- **Corporate pricing opacity.** The Corporate product has no published pricing or indicative limits, requiring full broker engagement for every placement. This slows the sales cycle for large accounts.
- **Healthcare and public sector exclusions.** Excluding healthcare and public administration from the Corporate product removes two of the highest-risk, highest-need sectors from the addressable market.
- **Bryte dependency.** Single-carrier underwriting creates concentration risk. If Bryte tightens appetite or exits the cyber segment, Phishield's entire product suite is affected.
- **Premium rate pressure.** Global capacity increases and the ~11% rate decline in 2025 compress margins, particularly for Corporate placements competing against Lloyd's and international capacity.

### Market Fit

Phishield's optimal market fit is the **SA SME segment** (turnover below R200 million) where transparent pricing, Funds Protect bundling, and cyber-specialist credibility are decisive. In the Corporate segment, Phishield competes on specialist expertise and local CERT capability against both generalist SA insurers and international capacity — a narrower but defensible position for mid-market corporates (R200m–R1bn turnover) that value local incident response over global carrier scale.

The protection gap — low insurance uptake despite high breach costs — represents the primary growth opportunity. Brokers should position Phishield Business as the lowest-friction entry point for uninsured SMEs, and Phishield Corporate as the specialist alternative to generalist placements for mid-market clients with complex digital risk profiles.
