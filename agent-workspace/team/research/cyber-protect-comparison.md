## COVER SCOPE

| Cover Element | Cyber Protect Business | Cyber Protect Corporate |
|---|---|---|
| **Primary trigger** | Cyber Emergency (hacking, malware, ransomware, data breach, cyber extortion, DoS) | Cyber Emergency (same trigger set) |

| **Claim expiry** | — | 24 months from date of Cyber Emergency / Loss of Funds (unless subject to pending legal action) |
| **Subrogation rights** | — | Insurer may take over legal proceedings against responsible third parties |

> **Limit options:** Both products use a three-option indemnity/deductible matrix completed at proposal stage. Specific rand values are entered by the broker/underwriter at binding and are not pre-printed in the standard wording reviewed.

## TARGET MARKET

### Cyber Protect Business
- Designed for **small and medium-sized enterprises (SMEs)** operating in South Africa.
- Suitable for businesses across banking, retail, legal, and technology services sectors.
- Entry-level cyber insurance with a streamlined proposal form (single-page underwriting questions focused on endpoint security, email filtering, firewall, and web filtering).
- Minimum security baseline required: paid-for internet security subscription covering antivirus/EDR, network firewall, email security, and web filtering — all installed and up to date on all systems and access devices.
- Account verification service access confirms this tier is aimed at businesses regularly making EFT payments to vendors (i.e., operationally active SMEs with supplier payment exposure).

### Cyber Protect Corporate
- Designed for **larger or more complex organisations** with more sophisticated IT environments.
- Underwriting assessment is materially more detailed: the Corporate proposal process includes a full **Phishield Cyber Risk Assessment Form** covering network/perimeter security, email security, MDR/SOC capability, SIEM log retention, backup and recovery, physical security, employee awareness training, remote access controls, and third-party vendor management.
- Implicitly targets organisations that operate or are considering a Security Operations Centre (SOC), Managed Detection and Response (MDR) service, and SIEM solutions.
- Suited to businesses with regulated trust accounts (legal practices, property practitioners) given specific trust-account underwriting provisions.

## UNDERWRITING REQUIREMENTS

### Cyber Protect Business
Proposal form (V1 2026) requires confirmation of:
1. Comprehensive paid-for internet security subscription on **all** systems and access devices, covering:
   - Antivirus/anti-malware with real-time EDR
   - Network firewall (configured for inbound/outbound filtering)
   - Email security solution (phishing, malware, malicious attachments)
   - Web-filtering solution (blocks known malicious/suspicious sites)
2. Name of endpoint security vendor and product.
3. Optional: External Cyber Risk Assessment Scan (cost included in the compulsory Risk Mitigation Fee).
4. Compulsory **Risk Mitigation Fee** payable to Phishield UMA — separate from the insurance premium, not charged by the insurer.

### Cyber Protect Corporate
Full **Phishield Cyber Risk Assessment Form** (V1 2026) is required, covering ten sections:
1. General business and IT environment profile
2. Identity and access management (MFA, PAM, privileged accounts)
3. Data classification, backup, and recovery
4. Facility and physical security
5. Patch management and vulnerability management
6. Endpoint and device security
7. Network, perimeter, and email security (SEG, WAF, DMZ, RDP/SMBv1 controls, DNS filtering, sandboxing)
8. Employee awareness and security training (annual training, phishing simulations, role-based training, C-suite engagement)
9. Remote access and workforce security (VPN, MFA, BYOD management)
10. Third-party vendors and cloud services (due diligence, security audits, contractual data protections)

Additionally, Corporate underwriting may require:
- SOC/MDR details: service tier, OEM integration, root cause analysis capability, cloud/network/email/endpoint coverage, IR execution authority.
- SIEM log retention period and percentage of critical assets forwarded.
- Average threat response time.

An **External Cyber Risk Assessment Scan** (performed by CyberSafe Consultants on behalf of Phishield UMA) may be required, covering internet-facing IP addresses. The insured must authorise the scan and certify accuracy of submitted IP ranges.

## PRICING NOTES

Specific premium schedules were not available in the documentation recalled. The following structural pricing facts are confirmed:

- **Limit/deductible options:** Both products offer three indemnity/deductible combinations (Options 1–3). Rand values are set at binding, not pre-printed.
- **Risk Mitigation Fee:** A compulsory fee charged by Phishield UMA (Pty) Ltd on the Business policy. This is **not** an insurance premium and is not paid to the insurer. It covers the External Cyber Risk Assessment Scan and related risk mitigation services.
- **Premium collection:** Monthly debit order. If a debit order fails, the credit balance from a prior overpayment is applied to the first unpaid month. If collection still fails, the policy lapses at end of the last paid month.
- **Corporate pricing drivers:** Given the more extensive underwriting assessment (MDR, SOC, SIEM, third-party vendor controls), Corporate premiums are expected to be risk-rated against a broader set of security controls. Organisations with mature security postures (SOC, EDR, MFA, SIEM) are likely to attract more favourable terms.
- **Broker channel:** Both products are distributed via intermediaries. Premium and fee schedules are confirmed at broker/underwriter level and are not published in standard product documentation.
